## Expand fossil coverage

Goal: Cover all fossil tetrapods

Progress: Missing groups include almost all Paleozoic amphibian-grade taxa; fossil Anura
and Caudata; most nonmammalian synapsids; sauropterygians; mosasaurs; maybe some other
fossil squamates; most fossil Neornithes.

MDD relevance: none

Notes: Mosasaurs and sauropterygians seem like the most sensible next group to focus on.
