# How should the black-headed _Cacajao_ be classified?

_Jelle S. Zijlstra, June 2026_

Recent treatments of black-headed uacaris disagree on species limits and on which
population bears the name _Cacajao melanocephalus_. I currently follow
[Boubli et al. (2008)](/a/4772), but the nomenclature of _melanocephalus_ and the
separation of _ayresi_ and _hosomi_ both need further corroboration.

## Taxonomic treatments

[Hershkovitz (1987)](/a/4862) recognized a single species, _Cacajao melanocephalus_,
with two subspecies: _C. m. melanocephalus_ on the left (north) bank of the Rio Negro,
and _C. m. ouakary_ mostly on the right (south) bank.

[Boubli et al. (2008)](/a/4772) described two new species, _C. hosomi_ from the area
where Hershkovitz placed _C. m. melanocephalus_ and _C. ayresi_ from farther downstream
on the left bank of the Rio Negro. They designated a neotype for _C. melanocephalus_
from the right bank of the Rio Negro in Brazil and accordingly considered _C. ouakary_
to be a junior synonym of _C. melanocephalus_.

[Ferrari et al. (2014)](/a/19437) transferred the name _C. melanocephalus_ back to the
_hosomi_ population, leaving the right-bank population to be named _C. ouakary_. They
also treated _C. ayresi_ as a subspecies of _C. melanocephalus_. They designated a new
neotype for _C. melanocephalus_ from Venezuela, but without a detailed discussion of the
previous neotype designation.

[Figueiredo-Ready et al. (2013)](/a/58354) used the species designations followed by
[Ferrari et al. (2014)](/a/19437), while [Silva et al. (2022)](/a/58352) used those from
[Boubli et al. (2008)](/a/4772).

| Upper left bank | _C. m. melanocephalus_        | _C. hosomi_                     | _C. m. melanocephalus_            |
| Lower left bank | _C. m. ouakary_               | _C. ayresi_                     | _C. m. ayresi_                    |
| Right bank      | _C. m. ouakary_               | _C. melanocephalus_             | _C. ouakary_                      |

## Nomenclature

The application of _Cacajao melanocephalus_ depends on which neotype designation is
valid. Both designations are cursory and do not explain why a neotype is justified. The
designation by [Boubli et al. (2008)](/a/4772) is additionally problematic because the
proposed neotype, MN 68616, is from Brazil, while the original type locality was in
Venezuela.

I provisionally accept the earlier designation by [Boubli et al. (2008)](/a/4772) of MN
68616 as the neotype, placing _C. melanocephalus_ on the right bank. A reassessment of
the designations could return the name to the left-bank population.

## Molecular evidence

[Silva et al. (2022)](/a/58352) is the most comprehensive study of phylogenetic
relationships within _Cacajao_. Both mitochondrial and nuclear data show a clear
separation between right-bank samples identified as _C. melanocephalus_ and left-bank
samples identified as _C. ayresi_ or _C. hosomi_, indicating that they represent
separate species.

Within the left-bank clade, _C. ayresi_ and _C. hosomi_ are reciprocally monophyletic
but very close to each other. They may be separate species, but further study is needed
to confirm their distinction.
