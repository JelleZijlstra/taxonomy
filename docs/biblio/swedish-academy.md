# _Kungl. Svenska vetenskapsakademiens handlingar_

The proceedings of the Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences changed title several times.
The following outline draws on
[BHL](https://www.biodiversitylibrary.org/bibliography/50688) and
[LIBRIS, the Swedish national union catalogue](https://libris.kb.se/bib/8257935):

- First sequence
  - _Swenska Wetenskaps Academiens Handlingar_ vol. 1 (1739–1740)
  - _Kongl. Swenska Wetenskaps Academiens Handlingar_ vol. 2–7 (1741–1746)
  - _Kongl. Svenska Vetenskaps Academiens Handlingar_ vol. 8–17 (1747–1756)
  - _Kongl. Vetenskaps Academiens Handlingar_ vol. 18–40 (1757–1779)
- ser. 2
  - _Kongl. Vetenskaps Academiens Nya Handlingar_ vol. 1–33 (1780–1812)
- Annual volumes, 1813–1854
  - _Kongl. Vetenskaps Academiens Handlingar_ (1813–1821)
  - _Kongl. Vetenskaps-Academiens Handlingar_ (1822–1829)
  - _Kongl. Vetenskapsakademiens Handlingar_ (1830–1854)
- N.F.
  - _Kongliga Svenska Vetenskaps-Akademiens Handlingar_ vol. 1–35 (1855–1901)
  - _Kungliga Svenska Vetenskaps-Akademiens Handlingar_ vol. 36–38 (1902–1904)
  - _Kungliga Svenska Vetenskapsakademiens Handlingar_ vol. 39–63 (1904–1923)
- Series 3: vol. 1–25 (1924–1948)
- Series 4: vol. 1–14 (1951–1974)

The start of N.F. needs care: BHL starts the sequence at 1855, whereas LIBRIS dates vol.
1 to 1858. The date of an individual contribution should be checked separately from the
date range assigned to the series.
