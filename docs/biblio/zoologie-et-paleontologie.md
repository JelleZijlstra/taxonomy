# Zoologie et Paléontologie Françaises

Gervais's _Zoologie et paléontologie françaises_ bears the dates 1848–1852 but appeared
in twenty livraisons. Their contents and dates are only partly known. The database uses
1848 for the first two deliveries, 1850 for Tome I pp. 1–136, publication by 31 January
1853 for pp. 1–158, and provisional completion in 1853. The evidence and assumptions
behind these dates are laid out below.

## The work and its database treatment

The full title is _Zoologie et paléontologie françaises (animaux vertébrés), ou
nouvelles recherches sur les animaux vivants et fossiles de la France, par M. Paul
Gervais_. Each of its three tomes has a title page dated 1848–1852:

| Tome | Contents                                                                                                                                              | Extent                                                        |
| ---- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| I    | Enumeration and description of species, with their geographical and palaeontological distribution                                                     | viii + 271 continuously numbered pages                        |
| II   | Plate explanations and additional memoirs, including material prepared after the corresponding Tome I text; amphibians and fish are treated only here | Separate sections, some unnumbered; collation in the appendix |
| III  | Atlas, with genus names in the captions                                                                                                               | 80 plates                                                     |

The database distinguishes these components:

| Component                                           | Adopted date        | Database citation                                                                        |
| --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Livraison 1                                         | By 13 March 1848    | {France (Gervais 1848) (livr 1)}                                                         |
| Livraison 2                                         | By 26 December 1848 | {France (Gervais 1849) (livr 2)}; the label retains 1849                                 |
| Tome I, pp. 1–136                                   | By 23 November 1850 | {France (Gervais 1850) (livr 1-8)}                                                       |
| Tome I, pp. 1–158                                   | By 31 January 1853  | {France (Gervais 1853) (pp 1-158)}, published before {Animalia fossil (Pictet 1853).pdf} |
| Remaining material without a supported earlier date | 1853, provisionally | {France (Gervais 1852) (livr 9-20)}                                                      |

Pictet cites pages up to p. 158; the pp. 1–158 component assumes that the continuously
paginated text appeared in page order and was published before Pictet. It overlaps the
1850 component. Tome II's separately paginated explanations are dated independently. The
labels "livraisons 1–8" and "livraisons 9–20" are aggregate citations; the allocation of
individual leaves to deliveries remains partly unknown.

In this treatment, receipt dates provide external upper bounds, with the cutoff used as
the adopted date under the database convention. The 1853 completion date is an editorial
decision. The dates of plate explanations and names depend on which version of the text
or illustration circulated.

## Publication evidence

### Early deliveries and the extent of the 1850 text

The following table summarizes the evidence for the twenty livraisons.

| 2          | Plates 2, 8, 11 and 12; discussions of plates 2 and 11 explicitly reported                | Received 26 December 1848: [Comptes rendus 27:655](https://archive.org/details/ComptesRendusAcademieDesSciences0027/page/n658/mode/1up); {France-Gervais livr 2 (Geneva).pdf} and {France-Gervais livr 2 again (Geneva).pdf} |

| 9–10       | Individual contents unknown                                                               | Ten advertised in [Baillière's catalogue, p. 29](https://archive.org/details/101622972.nlm.nih.gov/page/n40/mode/1up), assigned to 1852 by NLM but without a verified issue date                                             |

The
[January–June 1851 _Bibliotheca historico-naturalis_, p. 8](https://www.google.com/books/edition/Bibliotheca_historico_naturalis_et_physi/KpAxzW7wOAYC?hl=en&gbpv=1&pg=PA8)
assigns **Tome I pp. 1–136, 32 plates and 64 pages of explanation** to livraisons 1–8.
Sherborn instead gives **pp. 1–144 and descriptions of 21 plates** as issued by 23
November 1850 ({Animalia-index (A-Aff).pdf}, p. lviii). The conservative 1850 extent is
therefore pp. 1–136. The 64 explanation pages equal the extent of the sections for
plates 1–30 in the completed Tome II. Their original delivery membership and textual
state remain uncertain.

Wagner's annual reports in the _Archiv für Naturgeschichte_ corroborate the main
sequence: [15:3](https://www.biodiversitylibrary.org/page/13706022), reporting on 1848,
mentions two deliveries he had not seen;
[17:6](https://www.biodiversitylibrary.org/page/6956581), reporting on 1850, records six
more covering non-marine French mammals and cites Tome I pp. 5, 29, 120, 125 and 132;
[19:5](https://www.biodiversitylibrary.org/page/7071476), reporting on 1852, records
fourteen. His report on 1849 ([16:9](https://www.biodiversitylibrary.org/page/6961092))
still mentions only two, but later cites plates 1, 3–9, more than the Geneva notices
assign to those deliveries. Plates 1, 6, 7 and 9 were thus known by the review's
publication in 1850, without a secure individual date in 1849.

Gervais's _Liste des ouvrages et mémoires_ says fourteen had appeared
([p. 1](https://archive.org/details/listedesouvrages00gerv/page/n4/mode/1up)). It is
dated March 1852
([p. 8](https://archive.org/details/listedesouvrages00gerv/page/n11/mode/1up)) and was
received on
[5 April 1852](https://archive.org/details/ComptesRendusAcademieDesSciences0034/page/n553/mode/1up).
It already prints 1848–1852 despite reporting the work as unfinished. Entries 11, 17 and
18 cite the explanations of plates 37–40 and 19, and plate 1. Plate 39 is also cited in
{Ziphiidae (Gervais 1850) (Annales)}, p. 11, and in the
[account of the Academy session of 7 April 1851](https://www.google.com/books/edition/Revue_et_magasin_de_zoologie_pure_et_app/V2tIAAAAMAAJ?hl=en&gbpv=1&pg=PA195).
The extent and textual state of the explanations then in circulation remain uncertain.

The notices agree on four plates per livraison, but reported text extents vary: eight
pages in Baillière, 2½ sheets in the completion catalogue, and sixty sheets for the
whole work in Gervais's prospectus. Baillière's count may be stale; "Paris, 1850" in its
entry is a bibliographic date. The allocation of text gatherings to deliveries remains
unknown. The plates themselves demonstrably appeared out of numerical order.

### Pictet and the text through p. 158

{Animalia fossil (Pictet 1853).pdf}, second edition, volume I, has a preface dated 20
December 1852. Its receipt, with the first livraison of its atlas, is recorded for **31
January 1853** in
[Comptes rendus 36:234](https://archive.org/details/ComptesRendusAcademieDesSciences0036/page/n237/mode/1up).
The receipt supplies an upper publication bound. Pictet cites these Gervais pages beyond
the established 1850 extent:

| Pictet, volume I  | Gervais, Tome I |
| ----------------- | --------------- |
| pp. 374–375       | pp. 143–144     |
| pp. 381–382       | pp. 150–151     |
| p. 383, notes 1–3 | pp. 152–153     |
| pp. 384–385       | pp. 154–155     |
| p. 386, note 1    | p. 156          |
| p. 387, note 1    | p. 158          |

158 is the highest unambiguous page reference located. Assuming publication in page
order, these citations support the adopted bound for all of Tome I pp. 1–158. They
establish Pictet's access to the cited text, possibly through advance proofs.

There is an unresolved bibliographic oddity. Pictet p. 411 cites "p. 220" for a falcon
from Montpellier, whereas the matching passage is on Gervais p. 238; p. 220 concerns
human history, and related falcon text occurs with plate 1. Meanwhile Gervais p. 200
cites Pictet's second edition p. 385, and p. 244 cites Pictet p. 444. On p. 199 Gervais
distinguishes these later remarks from his already published second chapter: "Depuis que
notre deuxième chapitre a paru". Pictet also mis-cites p. 133 for _Galethylax_ on p.
397; the entry is on Gervais p. 132.

Pictet p. 388 describes _Hoplocetus_ and _H. crassidens_, citing plate 20, and p. 385
mentions a new discussion accompanying plate 39. These are leads for dating particular
explanations. The dates of the _Hoplocetus_ entries on Tome I p. 161 and the remaining
explanation leaves remain unresolved.

### Completion: 1852, 1853 or 1854?

| Witness                                                                                                                                       | What it establishes                                                                                                                                                               |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [_Bibliotheca historico-naturalis_, July–December 1852, p. 101](https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10076743?page=103)              | Announces livraisons 9–20 and completion. The catalogue's own publication date remains unverified.                                                                                |
| [_Neues Jahrbuch_ 1853:819–820](https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10283863?page=885)                                              | Lists the full work under an 1853 heading: two text volumes, 80 plates, 100 francs. The issue date is unverified. The atlas volume number is misprinted "II".                     |
| [_Archives des sciences physiques et naturelles_ 26:233–240 (1854)](https://archive.org/details/archivesdesscie06unkngoog/page/n241/mode/1up) | Review signed "F.-J. P.", headed "1848 à 1854", says Gervais has just completed the work. It establishes completion by the review, but could be delayed; its month is unverified. |
| [_Neues Jahrbuch_ 1855:222–235](https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10283865?page=246)                                              | Review based on examination of the work; repeats 1848–1852.                                                                                                                       |

Internal evidence favors some text having been prepared in 1853. On
[p. 251](https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb10231396?page=263), the
_Pristichampsus_ account cites "Comptes rend. Acad. sc. Paris, t. XXXV, p. 376; 1853".
The matching passage is actually **XXXVI:376**, in Gervais's paper presented on **28
February 1853**
([session](https://archive.org/details/ComptesRendusAcademieDesSciences0036/page/n352/mode/1up);
[passage](https://archive.org/details/ComptesRendusAcademieDesSciences0036/page/n379/mode/1up)).
The exact page and subject make this stronger evidence than an undifferentiated 1853
citation, although advance access or a replacement leaf could explain it.

Other internal references are less decisive. Page 193 describes a paper as forthcoming
in the _Bulletin de la Société géologique_ for 1853; p. 200 cites {Mammalia marine
France (Gervais 1853)}, p. 150, whose issue date remains unknown. Page 263 cites the
August 1852 _Annals and Magazine_ and refers to the deceased Prosper Renaux, who died on
11 August 1852 ({Dinosauria France-early discoveries.pdf}, p. 11). These last two
references give a lower bound in August 1852.

**1853 is the provisional completion year**, chiefly on the February reference and the
contemporary full-work listing. The 1854 review may be delayed. Fourteen deliveries had
already appeared by April 1852. The plates, final text leaves and assembled volumes may
have different completion dates.

## Plate explanations and name dates

### Revised explanations and the 1850 mammal names

The
[Geneva review of plate 11, 10:245–246](https://books.google.com/books?id=jD5GAAAAcAAJ&pg=RA1-PA245)
identifies figures 10–12 as _Dichobune cervinum_, as does the atlas caption. The
completed explanation instead calls them _Lophiotherium cervulum_ and explicitly
abandons the earlier identification after renewed examination. How this revision was
distributed remains unknown. The dates of other names in this text and in the
explanations of plates 8 and 12 likewise depend on the versions that circulated.

Verified Tome I entries provide a practical 1850 attribution for six names:

| Name                           | Page                           |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------------------ |
| _Castor (Chalicomys) sigmodus_ | 22                             |
| _Lophiotherium_; _L. cervulum_ | 56                             |
| _Anchitherium Dumasii_         | 64                             |
| _Sus major_                    | 100                            |
| _Felis Christolii_             | 124; locality continues on 125 |

These entries contain diagnoses or illustrated indications
([ICZN Article 12.2.7](https://code.iczn.org/chapter-4-criteria-of-availability/article-12-names-published-before-1931/)).
The dates of the revised explanation and its other names remain uncertain. Separate 1849
papers remain possible earlier sources for other cases.

No original livraison wrappers were located in these scans. Comparison has covered
selected passages and preliminaries. OCR searches found no further explicit livraison
receipts in _Comptes rendus_ 26–39, or another first-edition notice in the 1848–1854
_Bibliographie de la France_. The search also covered Geneva volumes 11–13, 15–16, 20,
22–23 and 26–27. Further notices may remain, especially where OCR is poor.

Two physical-copy leads remain:

- [Sudoc 133069982](https://www.sudoc.fr/133069982), Montpellier BU Sciences,
  **18277-58-1** (RCR 341722106): _Zoologie française_, Hocdé / chez l'auteur, 1848,
  catalogued as `iv [-xvi] p. - p. 1-16`, with plates **2–5, 8, 10–12**. These match the
  first two deliveries' reported plates. The unusual pagination and possible early text
  state remain unverified; no online scan was found.
- [MNHN EST PAL 16](https://calames.abes.fr/pub/ms/Calames-20220624102736984169): six
  loose plates numbered 2, 3, 3, 4, 4 and 5. Delivery dates and the survival of wrappers
  remain unverified.

The most useful further evidence would be wrappers listing contents, a dated receipt of
livraison 20, actual issue dates for completion catalogues, or dispatch/accession
records. Comparing early explanations with completed copies could identify revisions;
the Hérault paper and explanations cited by Pictet also retain unresolved dating
questions.

## Second edition

The second edition appeared in 1859, apparently in one part, and is listed in the
_Bibliographie de la France_ for **25 June 1859**, no. 5743
([notice](https://babel.hathitrust.org/cgi/pt?id=umn.319510019169955&view=1up&seq=308&q1=gervais)).
Names already present in the first edition are sometimes cited to this edition.
